Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Investucation is a student project. Its code is public on GitHub at github.com/jakeh009-code/Investucation, and anyone can run a copy. This policy explains what information the Investucation code handles, and what happens to it.

The short version: there are no accounts, the project doesn’t collect your personal details, and your progress stays on your own device.

1. What’s stored on your device

Investucation has no server or database. Everything the site remembers is saved in your web browser’s local storage, on your device only:

  • your practice balance, the shares you own and the trades you’ve made
  • your last trade, shown on the dashboard
  • your currency choice, if you pick one
  • recent exchange rates, so pages load faster
  • whether you’ve already seen each first-visit tip
  • a Finnhub API key, only if you choose to add one yourself

Nobody else can see any of this. It isn’t sent anywhere, and it isn’t linked to your name or email. To delete it, clear this site’s data in your browser settings. This resets your balance to £100,000 and empties your portfolio.

2. No accounts, adverts or tracking

You don’t need to sign up, and the simulator never asks for your name, email address or date of birth. There are no adverts, analytics or tracking pixels, and the site doesn’t set cookies of its own.

3. Your currency

To show prices in your local currency, the site looks at your device’s time zone and language settings. This happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and your location isn’t looked up.

4. Outside services the site uses

To show live prices, charts and exchange rates, your browser connects directly to these services. Like any website you visit, they receive your IP address and basic browser information, along with what was requested (for example, the stock ticker you looked up). Each one handles that information under its own privacy policy:

  • Finnhub: stock prices and company names
  • Yahoo Finance: backup source for prices and company names
  • Frankfurter: daily exchange rates published by the European Central Bank
  • TradingView: the price chart on each stock page, which may set its own cookies inside the chart
  • Google Fonts: the fonts used on the site

Whoever hosts a copy of the site (for example GitHub Pages, or your own computer) may also keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested.

5. Feedback on GitHub

The project doesn’t have an email address or contact form. Feedback and bug reports go through GitHub Issues. Anything you post there is public and is handled by GitHub under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Please don’t include personal details. You can edit or delete your own comments at any time.

6. Young people

Investucation is made for teens and young adults. Because the simulator doesn’t collect personal information, you can use it without giving any details about yourself. If you’re under 13, please use the site with a parent or guardian’s permission. GitHub accounts, which you’d need to leave feedback, are only for people aged 13 and over.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), you have rights over your personal information. The project itself doesn’t hold any. To remove something you’ve posted in a GitHub issue, delete it on GitHub. If you have a concern about how your information has been handled, you can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

8. Changes to this policy

If the way the project works changes, for example if accounts are added, this policy will be updated along with the code. The date at the top shows the last change, and the full history is in the GitHub commit history.

9. Questions

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub or see the feedback page.
