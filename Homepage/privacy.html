Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information Investucation (“we”, “us”) handles when you use this website, and what happens to it. The short version: we don’t have accounts, we don’t collect your personal details, and your progress stays on your own device.

1. What’s stored on your device

Investucation has no server or database. Everything the site remembers is saved in your web browser’s local storage, on your device only:

  • your practice balance, the shares you own and the trades you’ve made
  • your last trade, shown on the dashboard
  • your currency choice, if you pick one
  • recent exchange rates, so pages load faster
  • whether you’ve already seen each first-visit tip
  • a Finnhub API key, only if you choose to add one yourself

We can’t see any of this. It isn’t sent to us, and it isn’t linked to your name or email. To delete it, clear this site’s data in your browser settings. This resets your balance to £100,000 and empties your portfolio.

2. No accounts, adverts or tracking

You don’t need to sign up, and we don’t ask for your name, email address or date of birth to use the simulator. We don’t show adverts, and we don’t use analytics, tracking pixels or our own cookies.

3. Your currency

To show prices in your local currency, the site looks at your device’s time zone and language settings. This happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to us, and we don’t look up your location.

4. Outside services the site uses

To show live prices, charts and exchange rates, your browser connects directly to these services. Like any website you visit, they receive your IP address and basic browser information, along with what was requested (for example, the stock ticker you looked up). Each one handles that information under its own privacy policy:

  • Finnhub: stock prices and company names
  • Yahoo Finance: backup source for prices and company names
  • Frankfurter: daily exchange rates published by the European Central Bank
  • TradingView: the price chart on each stock page, which may set its own cookies inside the chart
  • Google Fonts: the fonts used on the site

The company that hosts the website may also keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to run and protect its service.

5. If you contact us

The contact form doesn’t send anything to us directly. It opens your own email app with your message filled in. If you then send it, we receive your name, email address and message. We only use these to reply to you, we don’t share them, and we delete them once your question has been dealt with, unless we need to keep them for a legal reason.

6. Young people

Investucation is made for teens and young adults. Because the simulator doesn’t collect personal information, you can use it without giving us any details about yourself. If you’re under 13, please use the site with a parent or guardian’s permission, and ask them before sending us an email.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), you can ask to see, correct or delete any personal information we hold about you. In practice, that’s only emails you’ve sent us. To make a request, email us at the address below. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

8. Changes to this policy

If the way the site works changes, for example if accounts are added, we’ll update this policy and the date at the top before the change goes live.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email help@investucation.org or use the contact page.
