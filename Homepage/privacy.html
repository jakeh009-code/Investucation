Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 23 July 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Investucation.org (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you access or use our website and services.
By using Investucation.org, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name or display name (if provided)
  • Email address (if account registration requires it)
  • Browser type, device details, and IP address (for site analytics and security)
  • Learning activity and simulation progress (to personalize dashboard features)

We do not collect financial data, payment information, or sensitive personal identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Operate and maintain the educational platform
  • Provide personalized features like dashboards and learning progress
  • Improve functionality and user experience
  • Monitor platform performance and detect misuse
  • Display non-personalized third-party advertisements

We never use your data to offer real financial advice or targeted investment guidance.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Consent: You provide consent when registering an account or agreeing to this Privacy Policy.
  • Legitimate Interests: We process data to maintain and improve platform operations.
  • Legal Compliance: We retain information where necessary to comply with applicable laws.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or disclose personal information to unaffiliated third parties.
However, we may share non-personal information with:

  • Ad networks that serve contextual advertisements
  • Analytics tools for monitoring usage patterns
  • Law enforcement or regulators when required by law

All external providers are vetted to meet basic UK data protection standards.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Secure servers and encrypted connections (where applicable)
  • Limited access controls to account data
  • Regular monitoring of platform activity for suspicious behavior

Your account data is stored for as long as you maintain an active profile. You may request deletion at any time.

6. Young Users

  • Users under 13 must obtain parental consent before registering.
  • We avoid collecting unnecessary personal information from minors.

We follow best practices under the Online Safety Act 2023, prioritizing user protection and minimizing data exposure.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

  • Ensure platform functionality
  • Track general usage statistics
  • Prevent misuse

We do not use cookies for personalized advertising or cross-site tracking.
You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

8. Your Data Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request corrections or updates to your data
  • Withdraw consent and request deletion
  • Object to certain types of data processing

To make a request, contact us via the form or email listed below.

  • Continued use of the site implies acceptance of the updated policy.
