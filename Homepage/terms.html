1. What Investucation is

Investucation is a free student project that helps teens and young adults learn how the stock market works. You trade real US stocks at real market prices using £100,000 of practice money. It isn’t a company, a financial service or a regulated product. Its code is public on GitHub at github.com/jakeh009-code/Investucation.
